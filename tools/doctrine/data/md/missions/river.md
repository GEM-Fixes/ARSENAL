# Force a river line

**Teaches:** Cross wide on a screened front and dig in on the far bank before the counterattack.

**Map needs:** river, one bridge, fords. **Win:** The attacker holds a far-bank flag for the last third of the match.

## Sides
**Attacker:** Soviet Guards rifle division, 1944, company shape, fidelity 0.7. Heavy, fast main blow and bold exploitation.
  - Mass most forces and fire on the main blow where ground gives cover and observation.
  - On finding a gap, leaders push through on their own, even off direction.
  - Fix a town frontally with part; send the main blow round to its rear.
**Defender:** German battle group, 1944 (reported doctrine), platoon shape, fidelity 0.7. Aggressive counterattacks; dangerous to push into. German doctrine here is reported by other nations.
  - Counterattack a lost position at once, close behind the fire, at the flank of the break-in.
  - Build strongpoints round MGs, heavy mortars and an SP gun, firing all round.
  - One narrow main effort with the heavy weapons massed behind it.

## Doctrine
- **River crossing:** Lead companies cross by boat, reserves by bridge. Form boat groups early and keep tactical unity. Normally three platoons abreast, with weapons following once they land. Hold fire on the water, rally on prominent features, clear the bank fast. Advance guards may simply cross by surprise.

## What a good bot does
- **Crossings are chokes; several points, then rally** (tac.crossing)
  - Cover the far bank with fire before anything crosses.
  - Cross at more than one point when the map allows.
  - Infantry first, abreast; heavy weapons and vehicles after the far bank holds.
  - Rally on the first prominent feature; don't stop on the bank.
  - Lead elements take an undefended crossing at once rather than waiting.
- **Smoke on the enemy, timed to the move** (tac.smoke)
  - Located target in range: HE. Unlocated or out of range: smoke on it or its field of view.
  - Smoke the enemy weapon, never a curtain in front of own units.
  - Start just before the move, keep it up until cover: rounds = (gap / speed) / profile smoke life (vanilla about 60 s, RobZ grenade about 90 s).
  - Skip if it blinds own overwatch or a neighbour's lane. Wind moves no smoke in game, so doctrine wind rules drop out.
  - Enemy smoke: laid weapons keep firing on approaches; reserve moves up.
- **Go past the objective, then dig in** (tac.consolidate)
  - Don't stop on it: move a short way past (design 0.1 to 0.2 x rifle).
  - Bring mg and light AT up at once to cover front and open flanks.
  - If not advancing, set all-round defense and enter cover.
  - Clear the whole objective.
