# Forest road in winter

**Teaches:** A road-bound force in forest is ambushed in pieces.

**Map needs:** snow, dense forest, one road. **Win:** The column reaches the far road flag, or the defender cuts it into two or more pieces.

## Sides
**Attacker:** Soviet rifle regiment, summer 1941, company shape, fidelity 0.9. Larp lore, not doctrine: frontal and costly on purpose. Its own manuals asked for initiative; this persona plays the 1941 reputation.
  - Attack straight down the ordered axis; do not shift to a found gap. (larp lore)
  - Hold or counterattack; withdraw only on a direct order from the top. (larp lore)
  - Feed the reserve into a stalled attack rather than holding it back. (larp lore)
**Defender:** Finnish battalion, winter 1939-40, platoon shape, fidelity 0.8. Needs a mod with a Finnish army or a winter map; maps to no stock army. Finnish practice here is reported.
  - Use snipers heavily from hides, as reported of Finnish practice.
  - Ambush road-bound columns from the woods.
  - Cut a stalled column into pockets and destroy them one by one. (larp lore)

## Doctrine
- **Winter fighting:** Thin evidence, Soviet only. Villages and woods matter more in winter, troops move slower off roads, and nights last longer. Deny the enemy shelter. Ski troops bypass and envelop. Without time to dig, build snow parapets; ice and charges go on tank approaches.
- **Ambush:** Lay the ambush in close ground on the enemy’s approach, on both sides of the route but staggered so no one hits his own. Let the scouts pass; the leader opens with rapid fire, best shots on leaders, men told off for vehicles. Strike hard, take prisoners, and leave before he recovers.

## What a good bot does
- **Ambush in close ground, open on the main body, leave fast** (tac.ambush)
  - Two or more parties on both sides of the route, staggered so their fire does not cross into each other.
  - Hold fire until the main body is in close range; let the scouts through.
  - One unit opens the ambush, all fire at once; first targets are leaders, AT crews and vehicles stopped by mines.
  - Against tanks: mine or block the lead and rear vehicles first.
  - Strike, then leave by a planned covered route before the enemy can reply in strength; never chase.
- **In winter, shelter and roads decide** (tac.winter)
  - Value villages and woods more when choosing axes and held points.
  - Expect slow off-road flanks; plan shorter maneuvers.
  - Check tank routes before committing them.
- **Snipers hunt hidden weapons and leaders; watch roofs and tempt fire** (tac.counter-sniper)
  - Own sniper: forward or on a flank, apart from the platoon; first targets are MG crews, AT crews, officers and spotters.
  - Move after firing or after a miss; mark targets for MGs and mortars.
  - Against enemy snipers: watch roofs, towers and steeples; clear them early.
  - Find a hidden enemy with searching fire or a cheap unit moving to tempt a shot, then answer with HE.
