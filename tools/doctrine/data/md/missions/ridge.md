# Hold the reverse slope

**Teaches:** Defend behind the crest and counterattack troops that come over it.

**Map needs:** ridge, open forward slope. **Win:** Defender holds the crest-line flag to the end.

## Sides
**Attacker:** German battle group, 1944 (reported doctrine), company shape, fidelity 0.7. Aggressive counterattacks; dangerous to push into. German doctrine here is reported by other nations.
  - Counterattack a lost position at once, close behind the fire, at the flank of the break-in.
  - Build strongpoints round MGs, heavy mortars and an SP gun, firing all round.
  - One narrow main effort with the heavy weapons massed behind it.
**Defender:** British infantry battalion, 1944, platoon shape, fidelity 0.6. Careful and smoke-heavy; rarely overextends.
  - Screen every crossing of open ground; move once the smoke is thick.
  - Consolidate at once on each objective before the next bound.

## Doctrine
- **Reverse-slope defense:** When the enemy can watch the forward slope, put the main position behind the crest. Outposts hold the forward slope; heavy weapons and most troops wait behind and fire onto the crest as the enemy tops it. Woods give the same cover. The risk: lose the crest and you lose the view.
- **Counterattack:** A penetration is fought at once by the support, by fire or by counterattack, and it must land before the enemy organizes. It strikes his flank in maximum force, while fires across the base of the penetration stop more from entering. Pursuit past the old line is by fire only.

## What a good bot does
- **Hold behind the crest when the forward slope is watched** (tac.reverse-slope)
  - Main position just behind the crest, out of sight of enemy units beyond close range; weapons laid on the crest line.
  - One or two outposts on the forward slope or crest to see and warn; they fall back on contact.
  - Most of the force held back as a counterattack group aimed at the crest.
  - If the crest is lost, counterattack it at once: whoever holds it sees the whole position.
  - Woods count as a reverse slope; sit back from the wood edge, not on it.
- **Hit the flank before he organizes** (tac.counterattack)
  - Go at once, whole, at the flank of the penetration.
  - Fire across its base to stop more entering.
  - Stop at the old line. Beyond it, fire only.
  - Enemy armor seen near and able to reach it: keep the reserve, hold a flank line, and use tac.at-screen.
- **Machine guns in enfilade, crossing fire, no gap at the flanks** (tac.mg-defense)
  - Site MGs on the flanks of the position, firing across the front, not straight ahead.
  - Neighbouring MGs' fields cross; check each flank is covered.
  - Heavy MGs take the long fixed lines; light MGs fill the gaps near posts.
