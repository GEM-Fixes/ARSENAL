# Retake the village at dawn

**Teaches:** Consolidate quickly, and counterattack before the enemy has.

**Map needs:** village, orchards. **Win:** The defender has just taken the village. The attacker must retake it, and the defender must consolidate in time.

## Sides
**Attacker:** German battle group, 1944 (reported doctrine), company shape, fidelity 0.7. Aggressive counterattacks; dangerous to push into. German doctrine here is reported by other nations.
  - Counterattack a lost position at once, close behind the fire, at the flank of the break-in.
  - Build strongpoints round MGs, heavy mortars and an SP gun, firing all round.
  - One narrow main effort with the heavy weapons massed behind it.
**Defender:** US rifle battalion, 1944, company shape, fidelity 0.6. Methodical; strong with support weapons, slow without them.
  - Lead with fire: mortars and MGs on the objective before any rifle squad moves.
  - Put the main line on the reverse slope when the forward slope is under observed fire.
  - Counter infiltration with posts that hold alone and a reserve that strikes hard.

## Doctrine
- **Counterattack:** A penetration is fought at once by the support, by fire or by counterattack, and it must land before the enemy organizes. It strikes his flank in maximum force, while fires across the base of the penetration stop more from entering. Pursuit past the old line is by fire only.
- **Company defense:** Stop the enemy by fire ahead of the line, and meet those who arrive in close combat. Two platoons forward and one in support, positions covering each other in width and depth. Frontage runs from 500 yards in close ground to 800 to 1,000 in the open, about double an attack zone.

## What a good bot does
- **Hit the flank before he organizes** (tac.counterattack)
  - Go at once, whole, at the flank of the penetration.
  - Fire across its base to stop more entering.
  - Stop at the old line. Beyond it, fire only.
  - Enemy armor seen near and able to reach it: keep the reserve, hold a flank line, and use tac.at-screen.
- **Go past the objective, then dig in** (tac.consolidate)
  - Don't stop on it: move a short way past (design 0.1 to 0.2 x rifle).
  - Bring mg and light AT up at once to cover front and open flanks.
  - If not advancing, set all-round defense and enter cover.
  - Clear the whole objective.
- **Keep a support, spend it whole, rebuild it** (tac.reserve)
  - Exposed flank or unclear enemy: hold back more, up to two of three groups.
  - Hold while fires or other groups can restart the attack.
  - Commit whole, at once, at a flank or at weakness; never reinforce strong resistance.
  - Then rebuild one from relieved or stray units.
