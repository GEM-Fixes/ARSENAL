# Noise in front, attack on the flank, 1942

**Teaches:** Don't swing the whole defence to face the noise; keep a reserve for the flank.

**Map needs:** jungle or dense woods, one track. **Win:** The attacker takes the rear flag.

## Sides
**Attacker:** Japanese infantry, 1942 (reported doctrine), company shape, fidelity 0.8. Flanking and night attacks; at high fidelity it charges when losing. All doctrine here is reported by the US and UK.
  - Fake strength in front with noise and automatic fire while the main force goes round a flank.
  - Under a holding attack, send LMG and grenade patrols round the flanks into the rear.
  - Prefer attacks when vision is short.
  - When a group is losing, charge instead of falling back. (larp lore)
**Defender:** British infantry battalion, 1944, platoon shape, fidelity 0.6. Careful and smoke-heavy; rarely overextends.
  - Screen every crossing of open ground; move once the smoke is thick.
  - Consolidate at once on each objective before the next bound.

## Doctrine
- **Bait and deception:** Make the enemy show himself with a bait, such as a dummy, decoy, feint, noise or a weapon firing to provoke a reply, then kill what answered with a hidden weapon. Refuse his bait. Only named guns engage decoys and scouts, and the rest hold to the set range. Dummies work only if plausible and well clear of real works.
- **Infiltration and its counter:** Small, heavily armed groups slip through unwatched or "impassable" ground, often in darkness or fog, while the main attack holds the defender. They take the start line or strike from behind. The counter is self-contained posts that fight all round, used as pivots for reserves that hunt the infiltrators.

## What a good bot does
- **Bait to find him, hold fire against his bait** (tac.bait)
  - A cheap element shows itself or fires; hidden killers are laid where a reply would come from and hit it at once.
  - Only units named for it engage scouts and lone vehicles; AT guns hold to the set range.
  - Feint on a second axis; if it finds weakness it becomes the main effort.
  - Withdrawing: a few weapons keep firing to mimic the position.
  - Ambush in close ground or defiles: open close, keep a way out, never chase.
- **Pass small groups through unwatched ground; counter with all-round posts** (tac.infiltration)
  - Attack: send a small, well-armed group (SMG, MG, light mortar) along the unwatched route while the main attack holds the front.
  - Its targets are the enemy's rear: heavy weapons, AT guns, crews, and the line the main attack must cross.
  - Defense: every post can fire all round; do not pull posts back when infiltrators appear.
  - Use the reserve to hunt the infiltrators, pivoting on the posts.
- **Keep a support, spend it whole, rebuild it** (tac.reserve)
  - Exposed flank or unclear enemy: hold back more, up to two of three groups.
  - Hold while fires or other groups can restart the attack.
  - Commit whole, at once, at a flank or at weakness; never reinforce strong resistance.
  - Then rebuild one from relieved or stray units.
