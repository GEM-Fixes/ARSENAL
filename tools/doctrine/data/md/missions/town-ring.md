# Encircle the town, 1944

**Teaches:** Fix the town frontally and send the main blow round it.

**Map needs:** town, open approaches, roads behind. **Win:** The attacker takes the road flag behind the town and the town centre.

## Sides
**Attacker:** Soviet Guards rifle division, 1944, battalion shape, fidelity 0.7. Heavy, fast main blow and bold exploitation.
  - Mass most forces and fire on the main blow where ground gives cover and observation.
  - On finding a gap, leaders push through on their own, even off direction.
  - Fix a town frontally with part; send the main blow round to its rear.
**Defender:** German battle group, 1944 (reported doctrine), company shape, fidelity 0.7. Aggressive counterattacks; dangerous to push into. German doctrine here is reported by other nations.
  - Counterattack a lost position at once, close behind the fire, at the flank of the break-in.
  - Build strongpoints round MGs, heavy mortars and an SP gun, firing all round.
  - One narrow main effort with the heavy weapons massed behind it.

## Doctrine
- **Clearing a village:** A fire section suppresses the main street; clearing sections alternate through the houses, entering from the back, top floor downward. Don't encircle: an escape route onto a killing ground beats desperate defenders. Thin evidence, nearly all from one UK manual.
- **Assault guns and tank destroyers:** Assault guns go to the main effort to help the infantry through. Tank destroyers are poor in close combat against infantry and need their own all-round security and early warning of tanks. Both can help reduce bunkers.

## What a good bot does
- **Fire down the street, clear from the back, leave a way out** (tac.town)
  - Bypass with leading groups if possible; a following group clears.
  - Fire element suppresses street-facing windows; assault elements enter from the rear.
  - Assault sections alternate clearing and covering; fire element moves up in bounds.
  - Don't encircle: leave one retreat line and cover it with a fire element or tanks.
  - Defending: hold inside the built-up area, not on its edge.
- **Assault guns at the main effort, tank destroyers kept out of close combat** (tac.assault-gun)
  - Assault guns go with the infantry of the main effort, firing HE on the points that hold it up.
  - Tank destroyers wait in overwatch for tanks, away from close enemy infantry, with their own infantry or MG cover.
  - Either can fire on bunkers once enemy AT is suppressed.
- **One main effort, on the best-covered ground** (tac.main-effort)
  - Score axes by cover on the way in and by enemy strength; pick one.
  - Team: one bot or pair is main effort, the others hold or fix.
  - Bot: most maneuver groups on that axis; the rest press to split the enemy.
  - If a unit isn't stopped, it keeps going while neighbors are halted.
