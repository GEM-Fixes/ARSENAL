# Mission kits

Generated by tools/missions.mjs. Scenario settings are design values, not doctrine. Nothing has been played.

- [Hedgerow fields, Normandy 1944](hedgerow.md): Tanks and infantry clear one field at a time, each covering the other.
- [Hold the reverse slope](ridge.md): Defend behind the crest and counterattack troops that come over it.
- [Forest road in winter](motti.md): A road-bound force in forest is ambushed in pieces.
- [Encircle the town, 1944](town-ring.md): Fix the town frontally and send the main blow round it.
- [Noise in front, attack on the flank, 1942](jungle-flank.md): Don't swing the whole defence to face the noise; keep a reserve for the flank.
- [Break a bunker line](bunker-line.md): Fire on the embrasures and smoke the bunker's neighbours while engineers close in.
- [Force a river line](river.md): Cross wide on a screened front and dig in on the far bank before the counterattack.
- [Retake the village at dawn](lost-village.md): Consolidate quickly, and counterattack before the enemy has.
- [Night attack on a perimeter](night-raid.md): Short vision favours whoever is closest and quietest. MGs on fixed lines hold the perimeter.
- [Pursue a withdrawing enemy](breakout.md): Push a fast group past the rearguard. The withdrawal is always ordered, not left to auto-move.
